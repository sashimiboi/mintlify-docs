## What is Plumi?

Plumi is an **AI-powered data platform built for risk and fraud teams**. Unify your fraud and risk data, ask questions in plain English, and get instant insights—no more waiting weeks for your data team.

<Info>
**Ask. Analyze. Act.** — Get answers in minutes, not weeks.
</Info>

Plumi acts as a unified intelligence layer that sits atop your entire stack—connecting transaction systems, case management, fraud vendors, and compliance platforms into one cohesive system. No vendor lock-in, no rip-and-replace.

- **Rule Optimization**: Track which rules catch fraud vs. generate false positives

- **Deploy in Weeks**: Go live in weeks, not months
