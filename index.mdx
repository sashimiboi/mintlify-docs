## What is Plumi?

Plumi is an AI-powered data analytics platform that transforms how teams interact with their data. Connect your databases, ask questions in natural language, and get instant insights with automatically generated SQL queries and visualizations.

## Why Plumi?

- **Natural Language Queries**: Ask questions in plain English and get accurate SQL
- **Multi-Database Support**: Connect to PostgreSQL, MySQL, ClickHouse, BigQuery, Snowflake, and MongoDB
- **AI Model Flexibility**: Choose from Claude, GPT-4, Gemini, or Mistral
- **MCP Integration**: Use your data directly in AI assistants
- **Secure by Design**: Your data stays in your infrastructure
